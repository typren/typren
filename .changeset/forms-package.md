New package: form handling for static typren sites. A canonical submission model with server-authoritative validation, honeypot plus pluggable spam verification, a web-standard Request/Response handler that fans out to pluggable delivery sinks (webhook, HubSpot, Google Sheets, JSONL file) with per-sink isolation, and a progressive-enhancement browser submit helper. Credentials travel only as `${VAR}` environment references resolved at deliver time.
